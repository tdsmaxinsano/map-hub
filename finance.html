④ Invoice Converter (IIF)

Output is a cleaned tab-delimited .iif ready for import.

▾ Reconcile to bank (per-batch funded date + fee)

Authorize.net's Settlement Date is when they batch the day's charges. The bank gets a NET credit (after ~2.9% fees) 1–5 business days later.

Required: Funded date (from Chase) — this becomes each deposit's QB date.
Optional: Fee (or Net — they're linked). When filled, a negative fee line is appended per batch so the deposit nets to the actual bank credit. Leave both blank to skip fee lines (deposit posts at gross — you'll book CC fees as a separate journal entry).

Wipes Fee + Net inputs from every batch above (keeps funded dates). Use when you want a gross-only export and will book CC fees separately in QB.
