④ Invoice Converter (IIF)

Drop a raw .iif export (tab-delimited QuickBooks Intuit format). The converter strips bookkeeping cruft columns (!TRNS / TRNSID / TRNSTYPE / ACCNT / CLASS / MEMO / CLEAR / TOPRINT / ADDR1 / ADDR5 / PAID), drops rows with TAXABLE or negative AMOUNT, renames columns to Invoice Date / Customer / Invoice No / Due Date, and normalizes "Medicare" → "Medicare Part B.". Output is a cleaned tab-delimited .iif ready for import.
